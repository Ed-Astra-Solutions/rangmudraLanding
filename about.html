OUR STORY

Where traditional craft meets urban contemporary style

RangMudra was born from a deep reverence for India's centuries-old textile traditions. Founded in Bangalore, we are a studio dedicated to keeping hand block printing and eco printing alive.

Our name says it all: "Rang" — colour, and "Mudra" — the impression of a hand. Every piece we create carries both: the colour of natural dyes and the unmistakable mark of the artisan who made it.

We work directly with skilled artisans, ensuring fair wages and a sustainable practice that honours both people and planet.

SUSTAINABILITY

Every decision we make at RangMudra is filtered through a commitment to sustainability. We source fabrics from certified organic growers and colour them with dyes made from plants, minerals, and kitchen waste.

Our workshop waste water is treated and reused. Our packaging is recycled and compostable. We are not just making beautiful things — we are making them responsibly.

In a world of fast fashion, RangMudra stands for slow, intentional creation — pieces made to last a lifetime and beyond.

OUR TEAM

Carve a design is more than being pretty :)

Our team is a family of passionate artisans, designers, and heritage enthusiasts. Each member brings a unique skill set carried down through generations of practice.

We are committed to using only organic fabrics and natural dyes in every piece we create. Our partnership with local artisan families keeps traditional livelihoods alive.
