Our Commitment

Sustainability Commitment

At RangMudra, we recognize the importance of preserving our environment and contributing to a sustainable future. Our commitment to sustainability is woven into every aspect of our business, from the workshops we conduct to the textiles we create.

Eco-Friendly Materials

We prioritize the use of organic fabrics and surplus materials of high quality, reducing the environmental impact associated with textile production.

Traditional Techniques

Our block printing methods are not only traditional but also eco-conscious, using natural dyes and minimizing chemical waste.

BYOC Initiative

With our Bring Your Own Clothes policy, we encourage the upcycling of pre-loved garments, extending their life cycle and reducing waste.

Empowering Local Artisans

We support the local economy by collaborating with rural artisans, providing them with fair wages and sustainable work environments.

Educating for Sustainability

Our workshops not only teach the art of block printing but also impart the values of eco-friendly practices and the importance of sustainability in art.

Community Outreach

RangMudra is committed to educating and involving the community in our sustainability efforts, fostering a collective approach to environmental stewardship.

Continuous Improvement

By committing to these principles, RangMudra aims to not only create beautiful textiles but also protect the beautiful world we live in. Thank you for joining us on this journey.
